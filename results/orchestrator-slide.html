Adaptive Budget Orchestrator 1 hour · 8–10 graphs · goal: minimize the worst k

Distributes a fixed total budget across graphs online & measurement-driven: gives time to whichever graph improves most per unit time; stops a plateaued graph and reallocates its time to one still improving.

In one sentence: a marginal-gain resource-allocation scheduler that splits the work into small time slices and gives each slice to the graph currently dropping k the most per unit time, stopping a plateaued graph and reallocating its time to one still improving. It knows no graph by name → it adapts on its own to any new graph.
